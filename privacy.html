Privacy Policy

Effective 28 September 2026

This privacy policy applies to the Rancher app for mobile devices, together with any related services operated by Nick Mirchandani (collectively, the "Application"). Nick Mirchandani is hereby referred to as the "Service Provider".

Information Collection and Use

The Application collects the following information.

  • Account information: your email address (or the sign-in you use with Sign in with Apple or Google), the ranchername you choose, and your birth year. The birth year is used only to confirm that you are at least 13 years old. You may also choose to add your home state or country.
  • Profile information: an optional profile photo, an optional bio (your "ranch philosophy"), and the background icon you choose.
  • Content you create: your ranchings, including the restaurant, chain, or bottle, your scores, your written review, your tasting notes, and your food pairing. It also includes your reactions to other ranchings, the ranchers you follow, and any reports or feedback you send.
  • Device and usage information: a push notification token, so the Application can notify you about follows and reactions. It also includes usage analytics collected through Google Analytics for Firebase, such as the screens you view, the features you use, your device model, your operating system version, and an app instance identifier. Service providers may process your device's IP address to deliver the service.

To create an account you must provide an email address or a supported sign-in, a ranchername, and your birth year. Everything else is optional.

What Other Users Can See

Other signed-in users can see your ranchername, profile photo, bio, background icon, follower count, and ranchings. They can also see your reactions to ranchings and whether you follow or are followed by other ranchers. Your email address, birth year, home state or country, and push notification token are never shown to other users.

Cookies and Tracking Technologies

The Application or its third-party SDKs may use cookies, SDKs, pixels, and similar technologies to support functionality, analytics, or service delivery. Where required by applicable law, the Service Provider will obtain consent before using non-essential tracking technologies.

Location Information

If you allow it, the Application uses your device's location to show restaurants near you and to center the map. Your location is used on your device and sent to Apple's MapKit service to find nearby places. The Service Provider does not store your device's location. Location access is optional: if you decline it, you can search for places by name.

A ranching records the location of the restaurant you rated, not your own location.

Data Retention Policy

The Service Provider retains personal data based on its necessity for the stated purposes:

  • Account, profile, and content data: Retained while your account is active and deleted when you delete your account
  • Usage analytics: Retained for no more than 14 months from collection
  • Reports and feedback: Retained as long as needed to review them and keep the Application safe
  • Aggregated and anonymized data: Retained indefinitely, because it no longer identifies you
  • Data required for legal compliance: Retained as long as required by applicable law

Deleting your account: You can delete your account at any time in the Application under Settings > Delete Account. This permanently deletes your profile, profile photo, ranchings, follows, activity, and login. Copies held in service providers' logs may remain for a limited period before they are removed. You can also request deletion by contacting the Service Provider at rancherapp@gmail.com.
